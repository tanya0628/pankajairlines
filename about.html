About Pankaj Airlines

Making private jet travel accessible for everyday families in India

Pankaj Airlines was started with a simple idea — everyday families in India also deserve the comfort, time-saving and complete privacy of a private jet.

While most private jet operators charge very high rates, we keep our prices significantly lower so that more people can experience private flying for weddings, family trips, medical travel or special occasions.

We operate with just one carefully maintained private jet. This helps us control costs and pass the savings directly to you.

No large fleet. No unnecessary overheads. Just honest, affordable private aviation from New Delhi — made for people like you.
